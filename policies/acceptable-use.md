# Scope

> lead: Greypillar is built to be capable. This policy says where capability stops, for customers and for Greypillar's own agents alike.

## 1 Who this applies to {#scope status=planned}

> In plain words: Everyone who uses Greypillar under an agreement, and everyone they let use it. The same lines bind Greypillar's own agents. The notes in the margin help you read it. If a note and a clause differ, the clause governs.

1.1 This policy applies to every customer of {claim:entity.legal_name} ("Greypillar"), to each person who uses Greypillar through a customer's account, and to every agent a customer configures in Greypillar. In this policy "you" means each of them.

1.2 This policy is part of the Greypillar customer agreement, the pilot agreement and the AI Addendum. It adds to the restrictions in Section 2.1 of the Common Paper Cloud Service Agreement Standard Terms and Section 1.2 of the Common Paper AI Addendum Standard Terms, which also apply.[^1][^2]

1.3 Greypillar's own agents follow sections 2 to 6 whether or not a customer asks them to. No customer, and no person at Greypillar, can make an exception to section 6 for them.

1.4 The notes headed "In plain words" are reading aids, not part of this policy.

# The people you serve

> lead: Greypillar may write and act on your behalf. It never does so to deceive or harm the people on the other side.

## 2 Deceiving the people you serve {#deception status=planned}

> In plain words: You decide how an agent speaks and what it covers. You cannot use it to mislead anyone you deal with.

2.1 You set an agent's style, its limits and what it talks about.

2.2 You will not use Greypillar to deceive or mislead any person, including your customers, suppliers, staff and the public, about a material fact, about who is speaking, or about their own options. This includes creating false urgency for your gain and arranging true facts to imply something false.

2.3 You will not use Greypillar to make or publish fake reviews, testimonials, endorsements or engagement, or synthetic endorsements that are not disclosed as synthetic.

## 3 Agents say they are agents {#agents-say-so status=planned}

> In plain words: A Greypillar agent always says it is an AI when it writes to a person, and says who is responsible. You cannot switch that off.

3.1 Each Greypillar agent that writes to a person says that it is an AI agent and says who is responsible for it.

3.2 No setting lets a Greypillar agent claim to be a person or deny being an agent. You will not configure, prompt or edit an agent's messages to remove its disclosure, and you will not present an agent's output to a person as written by a human where that would mislead.

## 4 Impersonation {#impersonation status=planned}

> In plain words: No pretending to be a real person or organisation, and no accounts that pose as people to talk to others.

4.1 You will not use Greypillar to impersonate a real person or organisation, to borrow a real person's name, voice or likeness without their permission, or to operate accounts that pose as people and interact with others.

# What you may not do

> lead: The ordinary limits of any serious service, and the ones that matter most for AI that acts.

## 5 Unlawful use {#lawful status=planned}

> In plain words: Nothing illegal, and nothing that breaks sanctions or export rules.

5.1 You will not use Greypillar for anything unlawful where you or the people affected are, or to break sanctions or export controls.

5.2 You will not use Greypillar to make a decision that the law reserves for a licensed or registered professional, or a decision in a regulated capacity, without the human oversight and review the law and that profession's rules require.

## 6 Collecting and using information {#information status=planned}

> In plain words: Greypillar can collect what is open to the public, within each source's terms. No one may pass a barrier a source has put up, use material from a breach, or knowingly break a source's terms of use. There are no exceptions. And no one may be profiled in secret.

6.1 **Open access.** You may use Greypillar to collect information that is openly available to the public, within the terms of the source.

6.2 **A source's terms.** You will not use Greypillar to collect or use information in a way that you know breaches the source's terms of use.

6.3 **Technical barriers.** Greypillar does not pass, and may not be used to pass, a technical barrier a source has put in place, such as a sign-in, a paywall or a CAPTCHA.

6.4 **Never.** No setting allows Greypillar to be used: (a) by any unlawful method; (b) to exploit a vulnerability in someone else's system; (c) with credentials you do not legitimately hold; (d) to use material you know or suspect comes from a breach; (e) to capture private spaces or communications covertly; or (f) to impersonate a real person.

6.5 **No covert profiling.** You will not use Greypillar to build a covert profile of an individual, to track a person across contexts without a lawful basis, or to score a person's reliability without their knowledge and a lawful basis.

6.6 You will not submit personal data to Greypillar without a lawful basis for doing so, or submit data your agreement prohibits.

## 7 Harm to people {#harm status=planned}

> In plain words: Nothing that hurts people, puts lives at risk without proper safeguards, or targets the vulnerable.

7.1 You will not use Greypillar to: (a) threaten, harass, stalk or intimidate anyone; (b) create or distribute sexual content involving minors, or any content that sexualises minors; (c) plan or assist violence, terrorism or the creation of weapons capable of mass casualties; (d) discriminate unlawfully against anyone, including in decisions about employment, credit, housing or access to services; (e) exploit a person's vulnerability; or (f) operate in a High Risk Activity, as the Common Paper Standard Terms define it, without safeguards and approval agreed with Greypillar in writing.

## 8 Messages sent through Greypillar {#messages status=planned}

> In plain words: Messages sent through Greypillar must be wanted, lawful and honest about who sent them.

8.1 You will not use Greypillar to send unsolicited bulk messages, messages to people who have opted out, or messages that break the electronic marketing laws of the place where the recipient is. Each message identifies the sender and gives a way to opt out where the law requires one.

8.2 You will not use Greypillar for link schemes, paid or exchanged links, or manipulation of search rankings, platform rankings or engagement.

## 9 Protecting the service {#service status=planned}

> In plain words: Do not attack Greypillar, overload it or get around its limits. If you find a weakness, report it under the disclosure policy.

9.1 You will not probe, scan or test the security of Greypillar, interfere with it, overload it, or get around its limits, access controls or metering, except under the vulnerability disclosure policy at /legal/vulnerability-disclosure or with Greypillar's written agreement.

9.2 You will not access another customer's data, or use Greypillar to gain unauthorised access to anyone else's systems.

9.3 You will not use Greypillar to create or spread malware.

## 10 The rights of others {#rights-of-others status=planned}

> In plain words: Do not use Greypillar to take what belongs to someone else.

10.1 You will not use Greypillar to infringe or misappropriate anyone's intellectual property, privacy or other rights, or submit material you do not have the right to submit.

# If the policy is broken

> lead: The response is proportionate, explained and reversible. It never punishes by deleting what you hold.

## 11 Reporting misuse {#reports status=planned}

> In plain words: Anyone can tell us about misuse. A person reads each report.

11.1 Anyone may report a suspected breach of this policy to abuse@greypillar.ai. Security vulnerabilities go to the address in the vulnerability disclosure policy. A person at Greypillar reads each report and answers the reporter where they gave a way to reply.

## 12 What Greypillar may do {#enforcement status=planned}

> In plain words: We tell you first, unless harm is serious and immediate. We stop only the part that causes harm, for as long as needed. Your data and everything delivered to you stay yours and exportable.

12.1 If Greypillar reasonably believes this policy has been broken, it may: (a) ask you to stop or to fix the breach; (b) refuse or stop the specific action, message or agent involved; (c) suspend the part of the service involved, under section 12 of the customer agreement; or (d) end the agreement for a material breach under it.

12.2 Greypillar tells you what it found and what it did, with reasons, before acting, unless the harm is serious and immediate or the law forbids notice, in which case it tells you as soon as it can.

12.3 Greypillar does not delete your content or revoke what has been delivered to you because of a breach, and you may still export it, except where the export itself would continue the harm or break the law.

12.4 Greypillar may report conduct to the authorities where the law requires it, or where the law permits it to prevent serious harm to a person's life or safety, and in each case only as the Data Processing Agreement allows.

12.5 A customer may ask that an independent reviewer, agreed by the customer and Greypillar in writing, reviews the decision. The reviewer answers in writing with reasons within 15 days after receiving the facts. Greypillar pays the reviewer's fees. The answer binds neither party unless both accept it in writing. If no reviewer is agreed within 10 days after the request, or after the reviewer answers, the dispute terms of the customer's agreement apply.

## 13 Changes to this policy {#changes status=planned}

> In plain words: A material change is announced 30 days before it takes effect. It reaches your agreement only as your agreement allows. If it makes things worse for you, you can leave without charge.

13.1 Greypillar announces a material change to this policy at least 30 days before it takes effect, at greypillar.ai/trust and by notice to customers.

13.2 A change reaches an existing agreement only as that agreement allows. For the customer agreement, section 17 applies, including section 17.5 for a change the law requires and section 17.4 for a change that is materially adverse to the customer. For the pilot agreement, section 17 applies: the version current on its Effective Date governs the whole pilot unless both parties accept a change.
