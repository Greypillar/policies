# Greypillar policies

The legal and policy texts of Greypillar LLC, with their full history. Every change is a commit you can read and compare.

**State:** first published 15 November 2026. The policies take effect on the opening of greypillar.ai, which has not opened yet. The agreements are drafts, not offered to anyone yet. Every text is written by Greypillar and has not been reviewed by outside counsel; each says so in its record.

## What is here

| Agreements | Customer agreement · Pilot agreement · Order form · Data processing agreement · AI addendum · Billing and refunds | Drafts, not offered |

Agreements are built on the [Common Paper](https://commonpaper.com/standards/) standard terms, free to use under CC BY 4.0. Each agreement names the standard terms and version it uses. [FORMAT.md](FORMAT.md) describes how a document is written; greypillar.ai renders the same files.

## Reading a document

Some sections appear twice under one number. The one marked `until=opening` holds until greypillar.ai opens; the one marked `opens=opening` replaces it from the opening. `status=planned` marks something that is not running yet. `{claim:…}` is a sentence the website fills in from Greypillar's record of public statements.

## How a change is made

From now on, each change is proposed as a pull request and takes effect when it is merged. Material changes are announced before they take effect: a new subprocessor at least 30 days ahead. Each document carries a version, an effective date and a dated list of its changes. Follow changes through this repository's commits or the feed on greypillar.ai/trust.
