# How billing works

> lead: A charge is shown before it happens, stays inside the limit you set, and can be disputed without anything else stopping.

## 1 What this covers {#what status=planned}

> In plain words: These terms sit under your agreement with Greypillar. If they differ from the agreement, the agreement governs. The notes in the margin help you read them. If a note and a clause differ, the clause governs.

1.1 These terms are part of the customer agreement at /legal/terms and the pilot agreement at /legal/pilot (each, the "Agreement") between {claim:entity.legal_name} ("Greypillar") and Customer. Capitalised words have the meanings the Agreement gives them. If these terms and the Agreement differ, the Agreement governs.

1.2 The notes headed "In plain words" are reading aids, not part of these terms.

## 2 Who bills you {#who status=planned}

> In plain words: Greypillar LLC sends every invoice and takes every payment. A partner who sells you their own service bills you for that service themselves.

2.1 {claim:entity.legal_name} issues every invoice and receives every payment under the Agreement. A partner's own fees for its own services are billed by that partner under its own terms and are not Fees under the Agreement.

## 3 Currency {#currency status=planned}

> In plain words: Prices set in US dollars are charged in your currency at the same value. You see the amount in your currency before you agree to it.

3.1 US dollar prices are billed in Customer's currency at the same value. The Order Form states the currency of billing.

3.2 Where a price is set in US dollars and billed in another currency, the amount in the billing currency is fixed when the invoice is issued or the charge is shown, using the rate of the payment provider named at /trust/subprocessors at that time, and is shown to Customer before Customer accepts it. Greypillar adds no margin to that rate.

## 4 Taxes {#taxes status=open}

> In plain words: Prices exclude taxes unless the order says otherwise. If the law makes you withhold tax from a payment to us, you withhold it, send us the certificate, and the amount counts as paid. You do not have to pay extra to make up for it.

4.1 Fees exclude taxes unless the Order Form says they include them. Common Paper Standard Terms Section 4.2 applies to taxes Greypillar itemises on an invoice.[^1]

4.2 Where Applicable Laws require Customer to withhold tax from a payment to Greypillar, Customer may withhold the amount required, pays it to the tax authority on time, and gives Greypillar the official withholding certificate within 30 days after the payment. On receipt of the certificate the amount withheld counts as paid to Greypillar. Customer does not have to increase the payment to make up for the amount withheld.

4.3 Open: Greypillar's Thai VAT position and the Thai withholding rate that applies to each kind of Fee. Until the Thai tax review answers, Thai baht prices exclude 7% VAT, and the withholding tax is under review. For Australian dollar prices, the GST position is being confirmed.

## 5 How you pay {#paying status=planned}

> In plain words: Either we invoice you and you pay within the days on your order, or you let us charge your card or account. Automatic charges never start from a trial or pilot.

5.1 Customer pays by invoice or by automatic payment, as the Order Form or pilot Order Form states.

5.2 An authority for automatic payment covers only Fees under that Order Form within the Spending Ceiling. It never starts from a trial or pilot, and Customer may withdraw it at any time for future charges by notice, after which Greypillar invoices instead.

5.3 Greypillar does not store full card numbers. Card and bank details are held by the payment provider named at /trust/subprocessors.

## 6 Seeing a charge before it happens {#before status=planned decides}

> In plain words: Before work that is charged by use runs, you see what it is expected to cost. Your total spending cannot pass the ceiling you set without a separate yes.

6.1 Where a Fee varies with use, the Service shows the expected charge before the work runs, using the same rating rule as the final charge. Where the final charge would exceed the expected charge by more than the margin the Service shows with it, Greypillar asks again before continuing.

6.2 The Spending Ceiling and any Emergency Allowance on the Order Form limit Customer's total committed spending, as section 5.2 of the customer agreement provides.

## 7 Plan balance and money you add {#balance status=planned}

> In plain words: Your plan's balance resets each month. Money you add yourself never expires while your agreement runs. When it ends, we send back what is unused, to the way you paid, within 30 days. If that fails, you can ask for it at any time for 24 months.

7.1 A plan's included balance ("Plan Balance") resets each month, or as the Order Form states. The Plan Balance is part of the plan's Fees. It is not Paid Credits.

7.2 Paid Credits are amounts Customer adds by payment. Money Customer adds itself never expires while the Agreement runs. Paid Credits can be used only for Greypillar's services under the Agreement and cannot be transferred to another customer.

7.3 Use is charged in this order: promotional credits first, earliest expiry first, then the Plan Balance, then Paid Credits.

7.4 When the Agreement ends, for any reason, Greypillar refunds unused Paid Credits automatically, within 30 days after the end, to the payment method Customer used to add them. If that refund cannot be made, Greypillar tells Customer, and Customer may ask for it, to a payment method or account it names, at any time within 24 months after the end. Greypillar then pays it within 14 days after the request. Section 9.3 of the customer agreement says the same.

## 8 Trials and pilots {#trials status=planned}

> In plain words: A trial or pilot never becomes a paid plan by itself. If you go on after a pilot, the whole pilot fee comes off your first invoices.

8.1 A trial or pilot never turns into a charge by itself.

8.2 A free trial ends at the end of its period with no charge. Continuing needs a separate acceptance of an Order Form.

8.3 The pilot fee is credited in full against the first invoices for ongoing work, as the pilot agreement provides.

## 9 Renewals and stopping {#renewals status=planned}

> In plain words: Nothing renews unless you turn renewal on. If you do, a monthly plan renews each month until you stop it. Stopping takes effect at the end of the period you have paid for, with no charge for leaving. Before a longer plan renews, we remind you.

9.1 Renewal and ending for convenience follow section 13 of the customer agreement. No early termination charge applies.

## 10 Price changes {#price-changes status=planned}

> In plain words: A price rise is announced at least 30 days ahead. You can leave before it applies, pay nothing more, and get back what you prepaid for after you leave.

10.1 If Greypillar raises a price, Customer can leave without charge. Section 9.4 of the customer agreement sets out how.

## 11 When you get money back {#refunds status=planned}

> In plain words: Every case in which Greypillar pays money back, how much, and how fast. If a case is not here or in your agreement, fees for a period already started are not refunded, and you can stop at the end of that period.

11.1 Greypillar refunds in each case in the table, in the currency paid and, where possible, to the payment method used, within 14 days after the refund falls due, except that section 7.4 sets when unused Paid Credits are refunded at the end of the Agreement. A refund covers the amount Greypillar received and any tax it collected on that amount that it can recover. Where Customer withheld tax from a payment under section 4.2, Customer reclaims the amount withheld from its tax authority, and Greypillar gives Customer the documents it needs to do so.

Table: Refunds

## 12 Disputed amounts {#disputes status=planned decides}

> In plain words: If you think a charge is wrong, tell us within 30 days. We stop collecting that amount while we review it, and everything else keeps running.

12.1 Section 10 of the customer agreement applies. In a pilot, the same process applies to a disputed charge, and section 7 of the pilot agreement applies to the refund.

12.2 Customer is asked to raise a disputed charge with Greypillar before disputing it with a card issuer or bank. A card or bank dispute counts as notice of a dispute under section 10 of the customer agreement, and pauses nothing beyond the amount disputed.

## 13 Late payment {#late status=planned}

> In plain words: We charge no late fee and no interest. If an undisputed amount stays unpaid after two warnings, we may pause new work. What you already hold stays yours.

13.1 Greypillar charges no late fee and no interest on late payment.

13.2 Section 11 of the customer agreement sets out what Greypillar may do when an undisputed amount is overdue.

## 14 Invoices and receipts {#invoices status=planned}

> In plain words: Every invoice shows what it is for, the price rule used, and any tax, so you can check it line by line.

14.1 Each invoice states the Order Form, the period, each line with its quantity and the rate applied, credits applied, taxes, and the total. Greypillar keeps the record of each charge and shows it to Customer on request.

## 15 Changes to these terms {#changes status=planned}

> In plain words: A change is announced 30 days before it takes effect, and reaches your order only as the customer agreement allows.

15.1 Section 17 of the customer agreement applies to changes to these terms.

## 16 Language {#language status=planned}

> In plain words: The English text governs.

16.1 English governs; translations carry the same meaning.
