# How this agreement is built

> lead: The order form carries the details of one pilot. Common Paper's standard terms carry the rest. Greypillar's own terms change the standard terms by name.

## 1 What this agreement is {#what status=planned}

> In plain words: This is the agreement for a 30-day paid pilot. The notes in the margin help you read it. If a note and a clause differ, the clause governs.

1.1 The Agreement has two parts: (1) this Order Form, including the documents it attaches or refers to, and (2) the Common Paper Pilot Agreement Standard Terms Version 1.1 posted at https://commonpaper.com/standards/pilot-agreement/1.1 (the "Standard Terms"), which are incorporated by reference.[^1] If there is any inconsistency between this Order Form and the Standard Terms, this Order Form controls for that inconsistency. Capitalized words have the meanings or descriptions given in this Order Form or the Standard Terms.

1.2 The Greypillar Terms in sections 3 to 20 are "Other Changes to Standard Terms". Each names the Standard Terms section it changes.

1.3 The Data Processing Agreement at /legal/dpa, the AI Addendum at /legal/ai-addendum, the Acceptable use policy at /legal/acceptable-use and the Billing and refunds terms at /legal/billing, each in the version current on the Effective Date, are part of the Agreement. In the AI Addendum, "Agreement" means this Agreement. If the documents that make up the Agreement conflict, they control in this order, for that conflict only: (a) the Data Processing Agreement, for Personal Data; (b) the AI Addendum, for AI Services; (c) this Order Form, including the Greypillar Terms; (d) the Billing and refunds terms and the Acceptable use policy; (e) the Standard Terms.

1.4 The notes headed "In plain words" are reading aids. They are not part of the Agreement and do not change its meaning. English governs; translations carry the same meaning.

1.5 "Working Day" means a day other than a weekend or a public holiday in Thailand.

# Greypillar terms

> lead: A pilot is real work, paid for, with a result stated in writing. These terms make that hold.

## 3 Business customers, and an early service {#stage status=planned}

> In plain words: Pilots are for organisations, not consumers. Greypillar is early, and you know that when you sign.

3.1 Customer enters into the Agreement for the purposes of its business, not as a consumer. The person accepting for Customer has authority to bind it.

3.2 Customer acknowledges the stage of the service when the Agreement is formed. At the date of this version: {claim:status.public_service} {claim:status.customers}

## 4 What the pilot is for {#purpose status=planned}

> In plain words: The pilot is real work. You may use what it delivers in your business, not only to test us. A person reviews every result before you get it.

4.1 This section changes Standard Terms Sections 1.1, 1.2 and 8.8.

4.2 "Evaluation Purposes" means Customer's use of the Product and the results during the Pilot Period to decide whether to enter into a Definitive Agreement, and Customer's use of the Delivered Materials in its ordinary business, during and after the Pilot Period.

4.3 A person at Greypillar reviews each result before it is delivered to Customer.

4.4 A "Definitive Agreement" is the Greypillar customer agreement at /legal/terms with an Order Form, or another agreement the parties sign for ongoing work.

## 5 Who answers for the work {#who-answers status=planned}

> In plain words: Greypillar answers for the pilot within the written scope, and the order names the role that answers for it. Acts the law reserves for licensed professionals stay with them. Your decisions stay yours.

5.1 The pilot is work Greypillar runs. Greypillar answers to Customer for the Stated Result within the Written Scope, and the Accountable Lead is Customer's point of contact for it.

5.2 Greypillar does not perform any act that Applicable Laws reserve for a licensed or registered professional, such as lodging a customs declaration, giving legal or tax advice where that is a licensed activity, or certifying conformity. Where such an act is needed, a professional licensed for it, engaged by Customer, performs and signs it and is responsible for it.

5.3 Customer keeps every decision that is Customer's to make, its relationships with its suppliers, brokers and authorities, and responsibility for every declaration, filing and payment made in its name. Greypillar does not represent that the pilot makes Customer compliant with any law, regulation or standard.

5.4 Each Greypillar agent that writes to a person says that it is an AI agent and says who is responsible for it. Customer may ask for a person at Greypillar at any time, and the Accountable Lead or another person replies.

5.5 Greypillar will not, on Customer's behalf, send, pay, commit, delete or file anything that cannot be undone, without the approval of a person Customer has authorised, given for that step or in advance within limits stated in the Written Scope.

## 6 Fee and credit {#fee status=planned}

> In plain words: You pay once for the pilot. If you go on with us, the whole fee comes off your first invoices for ongoing work. Nothing renews or charges you after the pilot unless you sign for it.

6.1 Customer pays the Fees under the Payment Process. Greypillar starts the work on the Start Date.

6.2 If the parties enter into a Definitive Agreement, Greypillar credits the full Fees paid for the pilot, excluding taxes, against the first invoices under it until the credit is used. The credit has no time limit: it applies whenever Customer enters into a Definitive Agreement. The credit does not apply to Fees Greypillar has refunded.

6.3 The pilot never turns into a charge by itself. When the Pilot Period ends, nothing renews and no further charge arises unless a person authorised by Customer signs a Definitive Agreement.

6.4 Greypillar charges nothing beyond the Fees and taxes for the pilot, unless a person authorised by Customer approves a further charge in writing before the work it covers starts.

## 7 Refund if the stated result is not delivered {#refund status=planned decides}

> In plain words: If we do not deliver what we wrote down by the date we wrote down, you get the pilot fee back, if you claim within 30 days after that date. You keep whatever we delivered. If you were late with your part, the date moves by the same time.

7.1 If Greypillar does not deliver the Stated Result by the Stated Date, judged against the Written Scope, Customer is refunded as this section 7 sets out, if it claims the refund within 30 days after the Stated Date.

7.2 The judgment is made against the Written Scope only. It does not depend on business outcomes or on the success measures, unless the Order Form lists a measure as part of the Stated Result.

7.3 To claim the refund, Customer notifies Greypillar within 30 days after the Stated Date, saying which part of the Stated Result was not delivered by the Stated Date.

7.4 Within 14 days after the notice, Greypillar refunds the Fees it received, and any tax it collected on them that it can recover, unless within those 14 days it shows Customer in writing, from the record of the pilot, that the Stated Result was delivered as the Written Scope describes by the Stated Date. Where Applicable Laws required Customer to withhold tax from the Fees, Customer reclaims the amount withheld from its tax authority, and Greypillar gives Customer the documents it needs to do so.

7.5 If Customer disagrees with Greypillar's showing, either party may ask that an independent reviewer, agreed by both parties in writing, reviews it. The reviewer answers in writing with reasons within 15 days after receiving the record. Greypillar pays the reviewer's fees. The answer binds neither party unless both accept it in writing. If the parties have not agreed on a reviewer within 10 days after the request, or after the reviewer answers, either party may proceed under section 18. A claim for the refund is a small money claim under section 18.4.

7.6 If Customer does not provide an input by the date the Written Scope sets for it, and Greypillar told Customer in writing at the time what was missing, the Stated Date moves by the number of days of that delay.

7.7 A Force Majeure Event does not excuse the refund. It only excuses Greypillar from liability beyond it.

7.8 Customer keeps everything delivered, including after a refund.

## 8 Customer's part {#customer-part status=planned}

> In plain words: You give us the inputs the scope lists, on time, and tell us if anything is wrong. If you cannot, say so, and we adjust together.

8.1 Customer provides the inputs, access and answers the Written Scope lists, by the dates it sets, and has the rights needed for Greypillar to use them as the Agreement allows.

8.2 Customer tells Greypillar promptly if a result appears wrong. Greypillar corrects a result that does not meet the Written Scope at no extra charge.

## 9 Your content, and what Greypillar learns {#content status=planned}

> In plain words: Your content is used only for your pilot. We do not train models on it. We keep only counts and timings that contain none of your content and cannot identify you.

9.1 This section replaces Standard Terms Sections 1.4 and 1.5.

9.2 Greypillar may copy, display, modify and use Customer Content only as needed to carry the pilot, to secure and maintain the Product for Customer, and as the Data Processing Agreement allows. Customer Content is kept in a store used only for Customer.

9.3 Greypillar does not train models on Customer Content. Greypillar will not use Customer Content, Input or Output to train or improve any model, except under a Contribution Grant made under the AI Addendum. Customer may choose to contribute data to improve Greypillar. Greypillar asks for each contribution specifically, says what Customer gains, and says what cannot be undone.

9.4 Customer may give Feedback, and Greypillar may use it freely. Greypillar may use data about the provision, use and performance of the Product that contains no Customer Content ("Usage Data") to provide, secure and improve its products, and may disclose it only in aggregate form that identifies neither Customer nor any User.

9.5 In work Greypillar runs, Greypillar may count Customer's results into totals across customers only as numbers that contain no Customer Content and identify neither Customer nor any person. Customer may opt out at any time by notice, without charge or change to what it receives.

## 10 What you own and keep {#ownership status=planned}

> In plain words: What we make for you is yours, and you can keep using it after the pilot, whether or not you go on with us. Greypillar's own parts inside it are for your own operations and your affiliates', not for resale. You may build anything with what you learn, even something that competes with us.

10.1 This section changes Standard Terms Sections 1.6, 1.7 and 2.3(a).

10.2 "Delivered Materials" are the results, reports, configurations, records and other materials Greypillar delivers to Customer in the pilot. As between the parties Customer owns them, except for Greypillar Components in them, and to the extent permitted by Applicable Laws Greypillar assigns to Customer its right, title and interest in them, except Greypillar Components.

10.3 "Greypillar Components" are software, rules, methods and materials Greypillar owned or licensed before the Effective Date or develops independently of Customer Content. Greypillar grants Customer a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free, perpetual and irrevocable licence to use, copy and modify the Greypillar Components that are in Delivered Materials, as part of them, for the own operations of Customer and its Affiliates only, and Customer may let any maintainer it chooses exercise this licence for it. The licence does not cover resale of Greypillar Components, or their use in products or services that Customer or its Affiliates offer to others. "Affiliates" are entities that control, are controlled by, or are under common control with Customer.

10.4 Standard Terms Section 1.6(iv) applies to the Product, not to Delivered Materials. Standard Terms Section 1.6(vii) does not apply, but Customer may not copy the Product itself.

10.5 Standard Terms Section 2.3(a) applies to access to the Product and to installed Software only. Customer keeps the Delivered Materials and may export everything it has received at any time.

## 11 Greypillar's promise about the work {#warranty status=planned}

> In plain words: We do the work with reasonable skill and care, to the written scope. Software features outside the scope are offered as they are.

11.1 This section changes Standard Terms Section 4.

11.2 Greypillar warrants that it will carry the work in the Written Scope with reasonable skill and care, and that each result it delivers has been reviewed by a person at Greypillar. Customer's remedies for a breach of this warranty include correction under section 8.2, the refund under section 7, and section 16. These remedies are not exclusive.

11.3 Standard Terms Section 4 applies to any part of the Product that Greypillar makes available outside the Written Scope.

## 12 Ending early {#ending-early status=planned}

> In plain words: Either side can end the pilot at any time by writing to the other. If we end it, or we break it, you get the fee back, as section 7 describes. If you end it for your own reasons, the refund rule above still applies to the date that passed.

12.1 This section replaces Standard Terms Section 2.2(c).

12.2 Either party may end the Agreement at any time by notice to the other.

12.3 If Greypillar ends the Agreement under section 12.2, or Customer ends it under Standard Terms Section 2.2(a) or (b), Greypillar refunds the Fees, in the amount section 7.4 describes, within 14 days.

12.4 If Customer ends the Agreement under section 12.2, the Fees are not refunded, except under section 7 where the Stated Date passed before the end.

12.5 On any end, Greypillar delivers the results produced to date, and section 13 applies.

## 13 Where data is kept, and when it is deleted {#data status=planned}

> In plain words: Your content is stored in Helsinki, Finland, unless the order says otherwise, and we reach it only from Thailand. If you do not go on with us, we delete it 90 days after the pilot ends, or sooner or later if you ask.

13.1 This section replaces Standard Terms Section 2.3(b).

13.2 Unless the Order Form states another location, Greypillar stores Customer Content in Helsinki, Finland, with the Subprocessor named for storage at /trust/subprocessors. Greypillar's personnel access Customer Content only from Thailand. Greypillar announces any new place from which it would access Customer Content at least 30 days before it is used, as the Data Processing Agreement provides. The providers that process Customer Content for Greypillar are listed at /trust/subprocessors or named in this Order Form, and the Data Processing Agreement governs changes to them.

13.3 If the parties do not enter into a Definitive Agreement, Greypillar deletes Customer Content 90 days after the Pilot Period ends or the Agreement ends, whichever is earlier, or sooner or later if Customer asks in writing, except what Applicable Laws require it to keep. Greypillar confirms deletion in writing on request. If the parties enter into a Definitive Agreement, Customer Content is held under it.

## 14 Your name and logo {#name status=planned}

> In plain words: We never name you, show your logo or quote you without your written permission for that use.

14.1 Greypillar will not identify Customer as a customer, use Customer's name or logo, or quote Customer or any User, in any material, without Customer's prior written permission for each use.

## 15 Confidentiality {#confidentiality status=planned}

> In plain words: Each side keeps the other's information confidential, as the standard terms say.

15.1 Standard Terms Section 6 applies without change. Customer Content is Customer's Confidential Information.

## 16 Liability {#liability status=planned}

> In plain words: Each side's liability is capped at the fees of the last 12 months, which for a pilot is the pilot fee. The cap never covers fraud, gross negligence, wilful misconduct, death or injury, or what the law says cannot be limited.

16.1 This section adds to Standard Terms Section 5.

16.2 Standard Terms Sections 5.1 and 5.2 do not apply to: (a) death, bodily harm or personal injury; (b) a party's fraud, gross negligence or willful misconduct; (c) Customer's obligation to pay the Fees, or Greypillar's obligation to pay a refund under the Agreement; (d) any liability that the law applicable to the claim does not allow to be limited. Where such a law allows a limit only to the extent it is fair and reasonable, the limits apply only to that extent.

16.3 Penalties, duties, taxes and charges that an authority imposes on Customer are Customer's own obligations. Where Greypillar's breach caused Customer to incur them, Customer's loss is recoverable as direct damages within the General Cap Amount, and Standard Terms Section 5.2 does not exclude it.

## 17 Changes to these terms {#changes-to-terms status=planned}

> In plain words: The version you sign governs the whole pilot.

17.1 The version of the Agreement and of each document it refers to that is current on the Effective Date governs the whole pilot. A change applies only if both parties sign or accept it.

## 18 Disputes {#disputes status=planned}

> In plain words: First, people from both sides try to settle it. If that fails, one arbitrator in Singapore decides. A refund or an unpaid fee can go to a small-claims court instead, so neither side pays for an arbitration over a pilot fee.

18.1 This section replaces the second sentence of Standard Terms Section 7.3.

18.2 A party with a dispute gives notice describing it. People with authority to settle it, from each party, meet or call within 15 days. If it is not settled within 30 days after the notice, either party may proceed under section 18.3 or 18.4.

18.3 Any dispute arising out of or in connection with the Agreement, including any question regarding its existence, validity or termination, shall be referred to and finally resolved by arbitration administered by the Singapore International Arbitration Centre ("SIAC") in accordance with the Arbitration Rules of the Singapore International Arbitration Centre ("SIAC Rules") for the time being in force, which rules are deemed to be incorporated by reference in this clause. The seat of the arbitration shall be Singapore. The tribunal shall consist of one arbitrator. The language of the arbitration shall be English.[^2] The law of this arbitration agreement is the law of Singapore.

18.4 A claim only for a liquidated sum of US$25,000 or less, with interest and costs, such as the Fees or a refund under section 7 or 12, may at the claimant's choice be brought instead in the courts, including small-claims courts, of the place where the defendant is incorporated or has its principal place of business. The defendant may not then require arbitration of that claim, but may raise in arbitration any larger claim of its own.

18.5 Either party may seek interim or injunctive relief from any court of competent jurisdiction. Standard Terms Section 7.4 continues to apply.

18.6 Nothing in this section limits the rights of a data subject, or the choice of law and forum in standard contractual clauses incorporated by the Data Processing Agreement.

## 19 Notices, assignment, and what survives {#notices status=planned}

> In plain words: Notices go by email or post to the addresses on the order. If Greypillar is sold or reorganised, its promises pass with the agreement. Your refund, your credit and what you own outlast the pilot.

19.1 Greypillar's Notice Address is {claim:channel.email}. {claim:entity.address} Customer's Notice Address is on its signature block.

19.2 Standard Terms Section 7.6 applies, and Greypillar may assign only to a successor that assumes all of Greypillar's obligations under the Agreement, including sections 6.2, 7, 10 and 13.

19.3 This clause adds to Standard Terms Section 2.4. Besides the sections listed there, the following survive any end of the Agreement: sections 6.2, 7, 9, 10, 12.3 to 12.5, 13, 14, 16, 18 and 19 of these Greypillar Terms, and every obligation to pay a refund that arose under the Agreement or the Billing and refunds terms.

20.1 Ongoing work after the pilot is governed by a Definitive Agreement. A Software License for running Greypillar on Customer's own systems, a service level agreement and an enterprise security addendum are not part of the Agreement.
