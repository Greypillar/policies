# How this agreement is built

> lead: A short cover page sets the terms that vary. Standard terms written by a committee of lawyers carry the rest. Greypillar's own terms change the standard terms by name.

## 1 What this agreement is {#what status=planned}

> In plain words: This is the agreement for using Greypillar and for work Greypillar runs. The notes in the margin help you read it. If a note and a clause differ, the clause governs.

1.1 This document is the Key Terms of a Cloud Service Agreement between {claim:entity.legal_name} ("Greypillar" or "Provider") and the customer named on the signature block ("Customer"). With the Common Paper Cloud Service Agreement Standard Terms Version 3.0 posted at https://commonpaper.com/standards/cloud-service-agreement/3.0 (the "Standard Terms"), which are incorporated by reference, it forms the Framework Terms.[^1] Each Order Form signed under these Framework Terms forms a separate Agreement.

1.2 The Greypillar Terms in sections 3 to 27 are "Other Changes to Standard Terms" under the Key Terms. Each names the Standard Terms section it changes. Where the Greypillar Terms and the Standard Terms differ, the Greypillar Terms control. Capitalised words not defined here have the meanings given in the Standard Terms.

1.3 If the documents that make up an Agreement conflict, they control in this order, for that conflict only: (a) the Data Processing Agreement at /legal/dpa, for Personal Data; (b) the AI Addendum at /legal/ai-addendum, for AI Services; (c) these Key Terms and Greypillar Terms; (d) the Order Form; (e) the Billing and refunds terms at /legal/billing and the Acceptable use policy at /legal/acceptable-use; (f) the Standard Terms. Despite the Order Form's statement that it controls over the Framework Terms, an Order Form changes these Key Terms or Greypillar Terms only where it names the section it changes, and then only for that Order Form.

1.4 The notes headed "In plain words" are reading aids. They are not part of this Agreement and do not change its meaning.

1.5 If a translation differs from the English text, the English text governs.

1.6 "Working Day" means a day other than a weekend or a public holiday in Thailand.

3.2 Customer acknowledges the stage of the service when the Agreement is formed. ## 4 Who answers for the work {#who-answers status=planned decides}

> In plain words: There are three kinds of responsibility. The software does what it is promised to do. When Greypillar runs the work, Greypillar answers for the result within the agreed scope, and your order names the role that answers for it. Where the law reserves an act for a licensed professional, that professional signs, not Greypillar. Your decisions stay yours.

4.1 There are three tiers of responsibility. Software: Greypillar stands behind what it is promised to do. Work Greypillar runs: Greypillar answers for the result within the agreed scope, through the Accountable Lead. Acts the law reserves: a licensed professional signs. The Order Form assigns each line of the Product to one of the following tiers, and Greypillar's obligations follow that tier.

4.2 **Software.** Where a line is in the software tier, Greypillar's obligations are those of the Standard Terms as changed by these Greypillar Terms, and the Product will perform materially as the Documentation describes.

4.3 **Work Greypillar runs.** Where a line is in the GP-run tier, Greypillar will carry the work in the written scope on the Order Form with reasonable skill and care, and answers to Customer for the result within that scope. The "Accountable Lead" is the role at Greypillar that the Order Form names for that line; it is Customer's point of contact for the result. A person at Greypillar reviews each result before it is delivered, unless the Order Form states otherwise for a named kind of result. If a result does not meet the written scope, Greypillar will correct it at no extra charge. Correction, and the remedies in section 9 and the Billing and refunds terms, are Customer's remedies for a result that does not meet the scope, without limiting section 20. These remedies are not exclusive.

4.4 **Acts the law reserves.** Greypillar does not perform any act that Applicable Laws reserve for a licensed or registered professional, such as lodging a customs declaration as a broker, giving legal or tax advice where that is a licensed activity, auditing accounts, or certifying conformity. Where such an act is needed, a professional licensed for it, engaged by Customer or by agreement under a separate engagement, performs and signs it and is responsible for it. Greypillar may prepare material for that professional.

4.5 Customer keeps every decision that is Customer's to make, its relationships with its suppliers, brokers, authorities, and customers, and responsibility for every declaration, filing, payment, and representation made in its name. Greypillar does not represent that using the Product makes Customer compliant with any law, regulation, or standard, or that it satisfies any obligation Customer owes to an authority.

4.6 Each Greypillar agent that writes to a person says that it is an AI agent and says who is responsible for it. No configuration allows a Greypillar agent to claim to be a human or to deny being an AI when asked.

## 5 Nothing irreversible without your yes {#your-yes status=planned decides}

> In plain words: You approve each irreversible step, once or in advance within limits you set. Past those limits it comes back to you. You also set a ceiling on total spending, and nothing goes past it without a separate yes.

5.1 Greypillar will not take, and will not configure the Product to take, an Irreversible Step on Customer's behalf without the approval of a person Customer has authorised. An "Irreversible Step" is an action of the Product or of work Greypillar runs that sends, pays, commits, deletes, or files something that cannot be undone within the undo window the Product states before the step. Customer may approve a step individually or give a Standing Approval on the Order Form or in the Product, within stated limits. A step outside those limits returns to Customer. If no authorised person answers, the step waits, unless Customer has named a fallback.

5.2 **Spending Ceiling.** The Order Form may set a Spending Ceiling. It covers Customer's total committed spending under the Agreement, including work that is running and charges not yet received. Greypillar will not incur a charge that would take Customer over the Spending Ceiling without a separate approval from a person Customer has authorised for that purpose. The Order Form may also set an Emergency Allowance that Greypillar may use, within its stated amount and purpose, before that approval, and Greypillar will report each such use within one Working Day.

5.3 Where a charge varies with use, the Product shows the expected charge before the work runs, using the same rating rule as the final charge.

## 6 Your content, and what Greypillar learns {#content status=planned decides}

> In plain words: Your content is used only to serve you. We do not train models on it. We keep only counts and timings that contain none of your content and cannot identify you. If you ever contribute data, you decide each time, in writing.

6.1 This section changes Standard Terms Sections 1.4 and 1.5.

6.2 Greypillar may copy, display, modify, and use Customer Content only as needed to provide, secure, and maintain the Product for Customer, to carry the work in the Order Form, and as the Data Processing Agreement allows. Greypillar will not sell Customer Content or use it for advertising.

6.3 Greypillar does not train models on Customer Content. Greypillar will not use Customer Content, Input, or Output, as defined in the AI Addendum, to train or improve any model, except under a Contribution Grant made under the AI Addendum.

6.4 Customer may choose to contribute data to improve Greypillar. Greypillar asks for each contribution specifically, says what Customer gains, and says what cannot be undone. A contribution happens only through a separate written Contribution Grant, signed or accepted for that contribution by a person authorised by Customer, in the form the AI Addendum sets.

6.5 In Standard Terms Section 1.4, "Usage Data" means only data about the provision, use, and performance of the Product that contains no Customer Content and from which no Customer Content can be reconstructed. Greypillar may use Usage Data to provide, secure, maintain, and improve its products. Greypillar may disclose Usage Data, or use it in marketing, only when it is aggregated with data of other customers and identifies neither Customer nor any User. The words "and promote" in Section 1.4 apply only to such aggregated data.

6.6 **Results counted across customers.** In work Greypillar runs, Greypillar may count Customer's results, such as how many checks passed or failed, into totals across customers, but only as numbers that contain no Customer Content and identify neither Customer nor any person. Customer may opt out at any time, without charge and without any change to what it receives, by notice or by the setting on the Order Form.

6.7 **No profiling of people.** Greypillar will not use the Product, or Customer Content, to build a profile or a reliability score of an identifiable individual, unless that individual has opted in. This does not restrict Customer's own lawful use of its own records.

## 8 Upkeep is in the service price {#upkeep status=planned}

> In plain words: When the world changes under work you pay us to keep running, keeping it working is our job and is already in the price, for the agreed period, scope and conditions. That covers changed connections, a retired model, fixes we owe, and agreed recovery. New purposes and new capability are new work.

8.1 When better AI arrives, Greypillar moves Customer's work to it, and upkeep is in the price, for the agreed period, scope, and conditions. Sections 8.2 to 8.4 say exactly what this commitment covers.

8.2 For each line of the Order Form that names an agreed result and an upkeep period, scope, and conditions, the Fees for that line include the work needed to keep the agreed result during that period, within that scope, and under those conditions. That work includes: (a) adapting to a changed interface of a system the line connects to; (b) qualifying and moving to a replacement when a model or provider the line uses is retired, degraded, or no longer meets the conditions in the Data Processing Agreement; (c) moving the line to a better available model where Greypillar has qualified it as keeping or improving the agreed result; (d) correcting defects and security issues Greypillar owes; and (e) agreed recovery after a failure.

8.3 Upkeep does not include new purposes, substantially expanded coverage, optional new capability, or work caused by Customer's own changes outside the Order Form, each of which needs a new or changed Order Form. The Order Form states any material exceptions and resource limits.

8.4 Upkeep covers Greypillar's work, not the price of AI compute. Where a change of model changes the compute charge for a line, Greypillar will show the expected charge before the change, and the Spending Ceiling in section 5.2 applies.

## 9 Charges, credits, and price changes {#charges status=planned}

Customer may then end the affected Order Form, or the whole Agreement, by notice given at any time before the increase takes effect, with effect on the date Customer chooses up to the date of the increase. ## 10 Disputed amounts {#disputed status=planned decides}

> In plain words: If you dispute a charge in good faith, we stop collecting that amount while it is reviewed. Everything else you pay for keeps running.

10.1 This section replaces Standard Terms Section 4.4.

10.2 If Customer disputes a Fee in good faith, it notifies Greypillar within 30 days after the invoice date or the automatic charge, saying which amount it disputes and why, with the evidence it has. Customer pays the undisputed amounts on time.

10.3 From the notice, Greypillar pauses collection of the disputed amount for a review period of 30 days, which the parties may extend in writing. During the review period, service that is otherwise paid for continues, and the disputed amount is not a ground for any pause or suspension.

10.4 If the parties have not agreed within the review period, either party may ask that an independent reviewer, agreed by both parties in writing, reviews the charge. The reviewer gives a written answer with reasons within 15 days after receiving the evidence. Greypillar pays the reviewer's fees. Collection of the disputed amount stays paused until the reviewer answers. The answer binds neither party unless both accept it in writing. If the parties have not agreed on a reviewer within 10 days after the request, or after the reviewer answers, either party may proceed under section 21, including by a small money claim under section 21.5.

## 15 The continuation test {#continuation-test status=open}

> In plain words: You can test that you could carry on without us. On your own installation, we switch ourselves off and you show that it keeps working. On our cloud, you leave with everything and an export, and another maintainer can take over from it. Your order sets when the test runs.

15.1 Once in each 12 months of an Order Form, and at the time the Order Form states, Customer may ask Greypillar to run a continuation test. In the test, Greypillar's access to the work under that Order Form is cut off, and Customer, or a maintainer it names, shows from the exported material and the Delivered Materials that the work can be continued without Greypillar.

15.2 For hosted service, the test covers export, the Delivered Materials, and handover to another maintainer. Running the work on Customer's own systems needs the separate Software License (section 27).

15.3 If the test shows that something Greypillar should have delivered or made exportable is missing, Greypillar supplies it within 30 days at no charge.

15.4 Open: the test procedure, its duration, who bears the cost of Customer's maintainer, and the limits for a test in the first year. Missing: a tested procedure on a real service. The procedure is written with the self-hosted licence, and this section stays open until then.

## 16 When a provider becomes unavailable {#provider-unavailable status=planned}

16.2 If a provider that the Product depends on becomes unavailable to Greypillar, Greypillar will qualify and use a replacement under section 8.

Greypillar then refunds prepaid Fees for the period after the end, pro rata, and unused Paid Credits. During the unavailability no renewal of the affected Order Form takes effect, and Fees for the part that does not operate stop accruing.
