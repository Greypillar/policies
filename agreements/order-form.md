# How to use this form

> lead: One form per order. Each blank in square brackets is filled in before anyone signs. A blank left empty means "none".

## 1 What this form is {#what status=planned}

> In plain words: This form sits under the customer agreement. It says what you buy, what it costs, who answers for it, and the limits you set. It can change the agreement only where it names the section it changes.

1.1 This Order Form incorporates and is governed by the Greypillar customer agreement at https://greypillar.ai/legal/terms, version [version], accepted on [date] between {claim:entity.legal_name} ("Provider" or "Greypillar") and [Customer's legal name] ("Customer"). In this Order Form, "Key Terms" means that customer agreement, including the Greypillar Terms in its sections 3 to 27, and "Key Terms section 4.3" means section 4.3 of it. The Key Terms incorporate the Common Paper Cloud Service Agreement Standard Terms Version 3.0 (the "Standard Terms").[^1]

1.2 Key Terms section 1.3 applies in place of the cover page sentence that gives this Order Form control over the Key Terms and the Standard Terms. This Order Form changes a section of the Key Terms only in section 10 of this Order Form, and only where it names that section.

1.3 Where a variable below is left blank, its value is "none" or "not applicable", and the clause that uses it does not apply, except where the Key Terms or this Order Form set a default. The commitments in the Key Terms apply in every case.

1.4 The notes headed "In plain words" are reading aids, not part of this Order Form.
